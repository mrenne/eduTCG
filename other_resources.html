Forge Your Own Game

Resources for Educators and Students to Design Custom Educational Trading Card Games & Board Games.

1. Educational Game Design Principles

Mechanics-to-Learning Mapping

The core idea of translating abstract concepts (like "Encryption") into tangible game mechanics (like "Damage Reduction"). Ensure every rule serves a learning goal.

Intrinsic Motivation (Malone's Principles)

Focus on design elements that encourage Curiosity, Fantasy (theme), Challenge, and Control to keep players engaged without external rewards.

The 4 C's of TCG Design

Design around **Collection**, **Creation** (deckbuilding), **Competition**, and **Community** to maximize engagement and long-term interest among players.

2. Digital & Physical Creation Tools

Card Maker Software (General)

Tools like **Canva** (for layout/visuals) or dedicated tabletop software (for mockups and testing) simplify the design and asset creation process.

Prototyping Materials

Use simple materials like index cards, sticky notes, and dice for the earliest phases of playtesting. Speed and low fidelity encourage rapid iteration.

Balance Modeling Spreadsheets

Create a central spreadsheet to manage all card stats (Level, Cost, Damage). Use simple math (like cost-per-damage ratios) to spot outliers and maintain balance integrity.

3. Playtesting and Iteration

Blind Playtesting

Hand the rulebook and components to new players without providing verbal instruction. If they can play successfully, your rule set is clear.

The "Fun First" Rule

If a concept is accurate but dull, prioritize making it fun and engaging. Educational value is lost if players quit before learning begins.

Feedback Loops

Incorporate **Feedback Cards** or simple victory conditions that track learning progress. Make wins feel earned and failures feel like puzzles to solve.
